## 3. Untapped Gaming & Visual Eras to Explore Later

### Era A — 16-Bit Amiga / Atari ST Demoscene & Isometric God-Game Era (1987–1993)
- **Why it fits Bandarchy**: The late-80s/early-90s European Amiga/Atari ST scene bridged arcade games and UK rave/acid-house culture (The Designers Republic, Warp Records, early VJing).
- **Signature Visual Ideas**:
  1. **Amiga "Boing Ball" & Raytraced Checkerboard Odyssey (1984–1989)**: A giant faceted red-and-white (or palette-duotone) geodesic sphere bouncing and spinning in 3D inside a mirrored floor/wall room, casting a soft grid shadow and echoing with raster copperbars.
  2. **12-Layer Parallax Alien Horizon (*Shadow of the Beast* / Psygnosis Roger Dean Style)**: Ultra-smooth 12-plane horizontal parallax scrolling with floating monoliths, organic airships, and a raster-split sky gradient.
  3. **Rotoscoped Flat-Polygon Cinematic World (*Another World* / *Flashback*)**: Stark, borderless flat-shaded 2D/3D polygons with dramatic diagonal rim lighting, laser shields, and lightning cavern silhouettes.
  4. **Vectorball / Brutalist Chrome Arena (*Speedball 2: Brutal Deluxe* / *Xenon 2*)**: Heavy industrial riveted steel & specular chrome spheres ricocheting through stars, warp gates, and electro-bouncers.

### Era B — Early-90s "2.5D" Flat-Shaded Polygon VR & Cyber-Trance Era (1991–1996)
- **Why it fits Bandarchy**: Before texture mapping became universal, 1991–1995 3D graphics (Argonaut Super FX, Sega Model 1, Namco System 21 *Starblade*, *Cybermorph*, *Virtua Racing*) used **pure untextured flat-shaded triangles** with crisp Lambertian face shading—which runs at 120 FPS in `QQuickContext2D` and looks ultra-clean.
- **Signature Visual Ideas**:
  1. **Super FX Low-Poly Canyon & Arwing Squadron (*Star Fox* / *Starglider 2*, 1993)**: Untextured flat-shaded triangular interceptors banking through giant low-poly stone arches, spinning polygon rings, and a segmented flat-shaded boss core (`Andross` cube/face).
  2. **Anti-Gravity Neon Ribbon Track (*Wipeout* / *F-Zero*, 1995)**: A banking, looping 3D roller-coaster ribbon track floating over a dark cyber-metropolis, with sleek twin-hull anti-gravity craft skimming over glowing speed-boost chevrons and設計師 (Designers Republic) typographic track pylons.
  3. **Synesthesia Wireframe Evolution (*Rez* / *Polybius* / *Child of Eden*)**: Kandinsky-inspired geometric wireframes and octree node networks that evolve their complexity stages (`Form 1 → Form 5` central cyber-humanoid or mandala) as track progress advances, pulsing with locked 8th-note target-lock reticles.

### Era C — 8-Bit UK Bedroom Coding & Psychedelic Surrealism (1982–1986 ZX Spectrum / C64)
- **Why it fits Bandarchy**: Distinctly British, eccentric, surrealist visual charm that complements the mischievous Cottingley Elves concept.
- **Signature Visual Ideas**:
  1. **Matthew Smith Surrealist Cavern (*Manic Miner* / *Jet Set Willy*, 1983–1984)**: Attribute-clash neon platforms, chomping toilets, spinning Victorian boots, giant pulsing Eugene heads, and conveyor belts operating in surrealist loops.
  2. **Ultimate Play the Game Isometric Vault (*Knight Lore* / *Alien 8*, 1984)**: Crisp monochrome/duotone isometric room blocks, spiked balls, cauldron bubbles, and drop-in ceiling hazards assembling and swapping rooms on bar drops.
  3. **Jeff Minter Llamasoft Herd (*Attack of the Mutant Camels* / *Gridrunner* / *Ancipital*)**: Giant multi-legged neon camels marching across a scrolling star-grid firing rainbow plasma bursts while laser matrices sweep X/Y axes.

### Era D — Pre-Digital Optical Parlour Toys & Mechanical Automata (1830s–1930s)
- **Why it fits Bandarchy**: Extends the tactile, hand-crafted aesthetic introduced by **Cottingley Paper Elves** and **Pinball**.
- **Signature Visual Ideas**:
  1. **Phenakistoscope & Zoetrope Spinning Slit-Discs (1833)**: Concentric stroboscopic cardboard discs where each ring contains 12–16 stepped paper-cut silhouettes (galloping horses, leaping frogs, dancing skeletons, blooming flowers) that animate via stroboscopic persistence of vision locked to the BPM.
  2. **Lotte Reiniger Silhouette Filigree Theatre (1926 *The Adventures of Prince Achmed*)**: Intricate black filigree lace-cut paper silhouettes with articulated pin-joints performing over backlit stained-glass tissue-paper skies.
  3. **Victorian Clockwork Orrery & Brass Automaton Gears**: Interlocking bevelled brass escapement wheels, pendulum anchor ticks, and coiled mainsprings that wind up during musical breakdowns and release in rapid mechanical cascades on the drop.
